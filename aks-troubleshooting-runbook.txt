AKS Troubleshooting Runbook

For CrashLoopBackOff:
1. Check pod status.
2. Describe the pod.
3. Review current logs.
4. Review previous crashed container logs.
5. Check Kubernetes events.
6. Review deployment history.
7. Check config maps, secrets, image version, environment variables, and resource limits.
8. Do not restart, delete, or roll back production pods until logs, events, monitoring data, and recent deployment changes are reviewed.
